Mahnoor Tariq teaches anatomy and writes its study material on
devsyllabus, from regional guides to nerve-and-plexus notes, all built
around the dissection sequence that Pakistani medical colleges follow.
Her writing favors tables and labeled drawings over dense narrative,
because that is the format the spotter and viva examinations actually
test.

She also covers microbiology and forensic medicine for Third Professional
MBBS students. Her aim in every piece is the same one she sets in the
dissecting room: the student should be able to reproduce the material on
a blank page, under time pressure, without the textbook in front of
them.
