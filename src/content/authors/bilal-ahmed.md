Bilal Ahmed writes the pre-clinical content on devsyllabus, covering
physiology, biochemistry, and pathology for First and Second Professional
MBBS. His approach is mechanism-first: every note starts from a normal
process and shows exactly where and how disease diverts it, the same
reasoning chain that examiners reward in one-best-answer questions.

He is also the author of the exam-preparation series on this site, which
treats MCQ technique as a learnable skill rather than an afterthought.
His work is grounded in standard texts, Guyton and Hall, Harper's
Illustrated Biochemistry, and Robbins and Cotran, and is written for the
Pakistani professional examination pattern. Before each note is
published, he checks that a student who only studies that note could
still answer the exam questions it targets.
