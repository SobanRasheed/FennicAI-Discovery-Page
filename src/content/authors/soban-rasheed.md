Soban Rasheed is a final-year MBBS student who founded devsyllabus while
working through his clinical postings. The site began as his own
structured notes, written because he could not find material organized
around the Pakistani professional examination pattern, and it now carries
the study notes, topic hubs, and practice questions that he and the other
authors wish had existed in first year.

He writes about study technique from direct experience: the spaced
schedules, flashcard systems, and recall methods described in his
articles are the ones he used for his own professional examinations. He
also authors the pharmacology and community medicine notes, favoring
drug lists and worked calculations, the formats those exams actually
reward.
