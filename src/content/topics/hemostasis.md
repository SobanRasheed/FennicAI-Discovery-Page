    answer: >
      Prothrombin time assesses the extrinsic and common pathways and is

  - question: How does fibrinolysis work?
    answer: >
      Plasminogen is converted to plasmin, which digests fibrin into
      degradation products measured clinically as D-dimer. Tissue
      plasminogen activator triggers the process, and drugs such as

## What it is

Hemostasis is the process that stops bleeding from an injured vessel
while keeping blood liquid everywhere else. Primary hemostasis builds the
platelet plug: exposed subendothelial collagen captures platelets through
von Willebrand factor, activation releases ADP and thromboxane A2, and
aggregation follows as fibrinogen bridges GPIIb/IIIa receptors. Secondary
hemostasis reinforces the plug: tissue factor activates factor VII, the
extrinsic and intrinsic pathways converge on factor X, and the common
pathway generates thrombin, which converts fibrinogen to fibrin. The
system is restrained by natural anticoagulants, antithrombin, protein C
and S, and by fibrinolysis, in which plasmin digests fibrin into
measurable D-dimer. Defects at any step produce a recognizable bleeding
pattern, which is why PT and aPTT exist as separate tests.

## Why it spans subjects

In physiology, hemostasis is a chapter of its own, sitting beside the
[cardiac cycle note](/notes/cardiac-cycle/) in cardiovascular teaching,
because flow, shear, and endothelial health all shape clot formation. In
biochemistry, the cascade is a chain of serine protease zymogens, and
the vitamin K dependent gamma-carboxylation of factors II, VII, IX and X
is an enzyme reaction explained in [biochemistry](/subjects/biochemistry/).
In pathology, hemodynamic disorders examine thrombosis, embolism,
infarction, and disseminated intravascular coagulation, the catastrophic
failure of this system, building on events in the
[cell injury note](/notes/cell-injury/). The whole topic is inseparable
from [inflammation](/topics/inflammation-and-repair/), since the same
endothelial injury triggers both, and from
[diagnostic test accuracy](/topics/diagnostic-test-accuracy/), since
coagulation profiles are among the most interpreted lab panels in
medicine.
